📚 API ENDPOINTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

POST /api/cart/add
  Add product to cart

GET /api/cart
  Get user's cart

DELETE /api/cart/item/:id
  Remove product from cart

PUT /api/cart/item/:id
  Update product quantity

DELETE /api/cart
  Clear entire cart

POST /api/orders
  Create new order

GET /api/orders/user/:userId
  Get user's orders
